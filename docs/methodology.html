EVIDENCE, NOT ASSUMPTION

Investigation methodology

A logged shared fight can be verified. A complete real-world raid history usually cannot.

Identity comes first

Stable Lodestone IDs resolve current FFLogs identities, worlds and regions. Report-specific player actors are matched conservatively by exact current name and world, or independently verified historical aliases. A same-name actor with no world remains uncertain. Job-specific actors and Unknown placeholders elsewhere in a report do not imply participation.

Same report is not the same fight

A verified shared encounter requires exactly one accepted actor per target in that same fight's friendlyPlayers list. Both wipes and clears qualify. Report co-occurrence, matching dates, ranking entries or unverified statements alone do not establish shared instance participation. Current and historical encounter identifiers are checked; report zone labels alone are insufficient.

Discovery and missing coverage

Character histories, known uploader/guild collections and bounded public historical searches provide candidate reports. FFLogs' character index has pagination restrictions and may omit older, private, deleted or unlogged activity. An exhausted returned index does not prove comprehensive raid history. Additional reports are screened by player roster before requesting fight metadata. Every comparison states its reviewed scope and unresolved gaps.

Time and timelines

Absolute fight time is report start plus fight-relative milliseconds. UTC is preserved; local timestamps use the comparison's IANA timezone, including daylight-saving transitions. Date boundaries are inclusive start and exclusive end. Both character lanes share the configured normalized window; hatched intervals mean unobserved coverage, not inactivity. A first observed fight is not first-ever participation.

Four evidence classes

  • Verified: reliable identity and same-fight participation.
  • Candidate: a possible shared fight with unresolved verification.
  • Inconclusive: available evidence does not establish a shared fight.
  • Coverage unavailable: inaccessible, missing, capped or unobserved records.

Counts and session boundaries

Duplicate report/fight keys are removed. Different uploads of one physical pull can remain distinct evidence records. Shared session clusters group one report and one local calendar day; they are not guaranteed distinct physical sessions or exact instance entry/exit times.

Publication and privacy

Results are precomputed and reviewed. Only target-character observations, direct evidence links and sanitized aggregate coverage are published. Development source, collection tools, raw API responses, full unrelated report inventories and credentials remain private. No authenticated live-comparison backend is advertised.
