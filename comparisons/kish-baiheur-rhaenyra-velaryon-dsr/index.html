Methodology & coverage

Identity & same-fight verification

Lodestone IDs resolve current FFLogs characters. Exactly one matching name-and-world actor ID per target must occur in the same fight's friendlyPlayers list. Duplicate job/Unknown placeholders elsewhere in a report are not presumed present. Null-world or fight-level ambiguous actors remain candidates; aliases require documented external verification. Wipes and clears are included.

Discovery & historical encounters

Character histories seed uploader and guild collections. Date-bounded public reports expand the priority search. Current and legacy encounter IDs are validated against the API; fight-level encounter IDs, not report labels, determine inclusion. See the report for exact searched collections and caps.

Time & normalization

Absolute timestamps equal report start plus fight-relative offset. Local calendar boundaries are converted with the configured IANA timezone, including daylight-saving changes. Timelines share a configurable start, but no missing records are invented.

Evidence classes

Verified: reliable same-fight evidence. Candidate: unresolved identity or participation. Inconclusive: no shared encounter established. Coverage unavailable: inaccessible or unobserved records. Participant context is never promoted to API evidence.
