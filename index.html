FROM IDENTITY TO EVIDENCE

How it works

  1. Identify charactersResolve stable Lodestone identities, worlds, and regions.
  2. Select an encounterValidate current and historical encounter identifiers.
  3. Choose a periodAlign calendar boundaries in a canonical timezone.
  4. Discover reportsSearch character histories, uploaders, guilds, and accessible public collections.
  5. Verify participationMatch report-specific player actors inside the same fight, wipes included.
  6. Publish a comparisonPreserve evidence links, audit trails, and explicit coverage limitations.

CONFIDENCE, NOT CONJECTURE

What the evidence means

Verified

Conservatively resolved identities appear in the same encounter's fight participant list.

Candidate

A potential shared fight needs additional identity or participation corroboration. Matching dates alone are not proof.

Inconclusive

The examined evidence does not establish a shared encounter. This is not proof of no shared raids.

Coverage unavailable

Historical data was not returned, is inaccessible, or was never logged. Unknown does not mean inactive.

Open by design

These are published, precomputed investigations, not live authenticated searches. No FFLogs credentials are sent to your browser. Development source and collection tools are private; only reviewed website files and sanitized evidence are public.
